💡 What Do These Metrics Mean? (Viva Reference Guide)
Total Lock Requests

Total number of distinct client attempts to acquire a lock across all workers.

Successful Acquisitions

Requests that successfully obtained exclusive mutual exclusion without clashing.

Released Locks

Locks that completed their critical section and were freed for the next waiting client.

Timeout Count

Requests where waiting time exceeded the client's timeout limit before lock became free.

Timeout Rate (%)

Percentage of total requests that timed out: (Timeouts / Requests) × 100.

Wait vs Hold Time

Wait Time: Delay before acquiring. Hold Time: Duration the lock was kept active.
