💡 What Do These Metrics Mean? (Viva & Defense Reference Guide)
Total Lock Requests
Total number of distinct client attempts to acquire mutual exclusion across all concurrent worker threads.
Successful Acquisitions
Requests that successfully acquired the OS kernel lock without encountering contention deadlocks.
Released Locks
Locks that safely finished their critical section and unlocked the kernel mutex for the next waiting client.
Timeout Count
Requests where waiting duration exceeded client's timeout deadline before the seat lock became free.
Timeout Rate (%)
Percentage of total attempts that timed out: (Timeouts / Requests) × 100. Key indicator of contention stress.
Wait vs Hold Time
Wait Time: Delay spent in queue waiting. Hold Time: Active duration spent executing in critical section.
